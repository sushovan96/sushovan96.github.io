Sushovan Mondal

Postdoctoral Fellow — Neutron Stars, Gravitational Waves & Pulsar Timing

I study how neutron stars ring. When a compact star is perturbed, it oscillates in characteristic quasinormal modes and radiates gravitational waves as it settles — my work is about decoding what those modes reveal about matter at nuclear densities.

01 About

I am a postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Physics at IIT Bombay, working on the physics of compact stars and gravitational waves. My research centres on the non-radial oscillation modes — f-modes, w-modes and beyond — of neutron stars and quark stars with realistic, anisotropic interiors, and on what these oscillations can tell us about the equation of state of matter at supranuclear densities. I also work with the Indian Pulsar Timing Array (InPTA) collaboration, searching for nanohertz-frequency gravitational waves in pulsar timing residuals.

Previously: Fellow, Institute of Mathematical Sciences (IMSc) · PhD in Physics, IISER Kolkata. — double-check these lines and dates before publishing.

02 Research interests

  • Neutron-star & quark-star asteroseismology
  • Non-radial oscillation modes (f, w, r)
  • Anisotropic stellar interiors & dense-matter EOS
  • Pulsar timing arrays & nanohertz gravitational waves
